    Reading the plan. Solid circle = where the actor is for this beat. Dashed circle and dashed line = where they started and the path they take. An arrow into a flap means an exit.

    Wings. The script's "left wing" is mapped to behind Flap 1 and "right wing" to behind Flap 2, as seen from the audience. "Foreground" is downstage, near the front stairs. Swap sides if your crew counts it the other way.
